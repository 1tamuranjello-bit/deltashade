Thank you

Thank you for your purchase

Your payment is complete. You'll receive two emails (in English). Follow these four steps to get DeltaShade and start using it.

Your license key

The key is also sent to you by email. Keep it private and safe. You'll paste it into the app to unlock it.

  1. Open the email

    Open the email from “Dodo Payments” and click “Access in your customer portal” (or “Access license”). If it doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

  2. Click the button on your customer page

    A page listing what you bought opens. Click the button at the right of the “DeltaShade Download” row (②); a panel opens on the right.

  3. Download

    In the panel on the right, click the small download button (③) next to the file for your computer: .dmg for Mac, Setup.exe for Windows.

  4. Check your license key

    On the same page, click the button at the right of the “DeltaShade” row (④) to show your license key and copy it.
